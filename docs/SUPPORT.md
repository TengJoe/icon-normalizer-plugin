## 简体中文

Icon Normalizer 是由 **[TengJoe](https://github.com/TengJoe)** 维护的免费开源项目，以 [GPL-3.0-or-later](../LICENSE) 发布。所有功能均可免费使用，支持开发完全自愿，没有金额要求。

你可以通过下方的微信或支付宝收款码支持开发与维护。请使用对应支付应用扫描，在应用内核对收款人和金额，再确认付款。收款码也可在插件的“维护 → 关于与支持”中查看。

遇到使用问题时，请到 [Issues](https://github.com/TengJoe/icon-normalizer-plugin/issues) 反馈，并参考[贡献指南](../CONTRIBUTING.md)提供复现信息。

## English

Icon Normalizer is a free, open-source project maintained by **[TengJoe](https://github.com/TengJoe)** and licensed under [GPL-3.0-or-later](../LICENSE). All features are available for free. Supporting development is voluntary, with no required amount.

Use the WeChat Pay or Alipay codes below to support development and maintenance. Scan with the corresponding payment app, verify the recipient and amount, and confirm the payment there. The same codes are available in **Maintenance → About and support**.

For usage problems, open an [issue](https://github.com/TengJoe/icon-normalizer-plugin/issues) and follow the [contribution guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md) when providing reproduction details.
